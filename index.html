happy 2nd mensiversary, sayang.

aku mencintai kamu, menyayangi kamu dengan sepenuh hati yang aku punya. i want to keep walking through life with you, facing everything together, creating beautiful moments, and making memories that we can look back on with a smile.

sayang, i love you, i love you, i love you. more than words could ever explain. aku bersyukur, saaaangat bersyukur karena semesta mempertemukan aku sama kamu. out of all the possibilities in this world, i’m so happy that i got to meet you, love you, and share my days with you.

terima kasih sudah hadir di hidup aku, thank you for being a part of my days, dan terima kasih karena sampai hari ini kita masih sama-sama memilih untuk bertahan dan melangkah bersama. i know our journey is still long. masih banyak hal yang belum kita lewati, banyak cerita yang belum kita tulis, dan banyak kenangan yang masih menunggu untuk kita ciptakan.

aku nggak berharap hubungan kita selalu sempurna, sayang. i only hope that we’ll always have reasons to understand each other, forgive each other, and hold on to one another when things get hard. kalau nanti ada perbedaan, semoga kita bisa belajar untuk mengerti satu sama lain. kalau nanti ada masalah, semoga kita nggak lupa bahwa we are two people fighting for the same love, not two people who have to fight against each other.

i want to grow with you, learn with you, and discover more sides of you as time goes by. aku mau menjadi tempat kamu bercerita, tempat kamu pulang ketika lelah, dan seseorang yang selalu berusaha membuat kamu merasa dicintai. i want you to know that your presence means so much to me, more than i could ever put into words.

happy second mensiversary, cintaku. two months may still feel short compared to the long journey i want to take with you, but every moment we’ve shared means so much to me. i will always cherish every laugh, every story, every little conversation, and even the simplest moments, because they are all part of our story.

i love you, sayang. today, tomorrow, and hopefully on many more days to come. aku mau terus memilih kamu, terus belajar mencintai kamu dengan cara yang kamu butuhkan, dan terus membangun hubungan ini bersama-sama. may we always have hearts that are willing to fight for us, hands that are willing to hold on, and the same goal of continuing to walk side by side.

here's to us, to our little love story, and to all the beautiful memories we're going to make together. aku sayang kamu, more than i could ever explain. always you, my love.

With Love,
Evgen
